First validated public Vietnamese checkpoint for the multilingual EGA/FGA/SGA program.

Current scope is deliberately narrow: **EGA II, Corollary II.7.3.10 and its proof** (French printed pages 146–147). This release does not claim a complete translation of EGA II or the wider corpus.

Validation gates:

- corresponding Brazilian-Portuguese unit already exists;
- sealed Vietnamese algebraic-geometry terminology canon admitted before translation;
- two clean LuaLaTeX builds are byte-identical;
- zero hard TeX, undefined-command, box or missing-glyph findings;
- structural/source alignment and 1/1-page visual review pass.

All release assets are public and accompanied by exact SHA-256 checksums.
